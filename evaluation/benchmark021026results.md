# Benchmark 2026-10-02 — Quadgram vs Char-GRU, 5-fold CV

## Setup

- Command: `evaluation/kfold_cv.py --lang both --k 5 --train-words 1000000 --max-lines 150000 --deltas 3,4,5,6,8,10,12,14,17,20 --symmetric --epochs 4 --jobs 11 --no-reports --out evaluation/kfold_gru_vs_quad.json`
- Run: 2026-10-02 13:03:55 – 14:29:00; code was commit `ae678ee` (CUSUM evidence) plus two then-uncommitted changes: the neural-path evidence and incremental scoring later committed as `f266b87`, and the k-fold harness rework (`--deltas`, `--train-words`, dedupe, shipped GRU hyperparameters). The PC slept for ~11 min during EN fold 2.
- Corpora: `data/he_corpus.txt`, `data/en_corpus.txt`; first 150,000 eligible lines read (`load_corpus_lines`, pure-script lines only), exact duplicate lines removed, then the line pool cut at the first point where it holds ≥1,250,000 whitespace words (1,000,000 × 5/4).
- Pool after dedupe and trim: HE 48,171 unique lines, EN 31,913 unique lines.
- Folds: line `i` is in test fold `f` when `i % 5 == f`. Per fold an assert checked that no test line appears in the training lines; it passed on all 10 folds.
- Symmetric: in each fold both the primary and the other-language model were trained on that fold's training split of their own corpus; no shipped model was used.
- Quadgram: `build_quadgrams_from_lines(train_lines, min_count=2)`, saved to a MARISA trie.
- Char-GRU: shipped hyperparameters from `data/{lang}_char_gru.meta.json` (emb 32, hidden 96, dropout 0.05, lr 0.004, weight decay 1e-4), 4 epochs, trained on all filtered words (length 2–14, model alphabet) of the training lines.
- Evaluation: `run_test` FP and FN tests, mode `standard`, 2 consecutive confirmations, evidence accumulation on (benchmark default, α 0.1). Both models scored on the same test lines at every delta.
- FP/1k = false switches per 1,000 words on correctly typed text. FN/1k = missed wrong-layout words per 1,000 words. Latency = the FN test's `latency_values`, in characters; mean and median are each averaged over the 5 folds.
